Magic Fight Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Magic Fight is a casual game made by Norm Julian. This policy explains what information the game collects and how it is used. The short version: there are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and the only information that leaves your device is what you choose to submit to the leaderboard.

Leaderboard

After a match you can optionally save your result to the online leaderboard. If you do, the following is stored:

  • the name you enter (up to 20 characters; it does not need to be your real name)
  • the characters played, game mode, and speed
  • whether you won, your best streak, and any lore unlocked
  • the date and time of the match

Leaderboard entries are public and visible to anyone who plays the game or visits its website, so please don't enter personal information as your name. If you skip saving, nothing about your match is sent anywhere.

Service providers

Leaderboard data is stored with Supabase, and the web version of the game is hosted by Vercel. Like most online services, these providers automatically keep short-term technical logs of requests, which can include your IP address. This information is not used to identify you and is not sold or shared for advertising.

Stored on your device

The game remembers a few settings on your device, such as whether you've completed the tutorial. If you choose to save a lore image, it is saved to a "Magic Fight" album in your photo gallery. This information stays on your device and is never uploaded.

Children

Magic Fight is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

To have a leaderboard entry removed, email normvjulian@gmail.com with the name you used and roughly when you played, and it will be deleted. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your device, except images you saved to your gallery.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
