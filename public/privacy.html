Magic Fight Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Magic Fight is a casual game made by Norm Julian. This policy explains what information the game collects and how it is used. The short version: it doesn't collect any. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and nothing about you or your games leaves your device.

Stored on your device

The game remembers a few things on your device: settings such as whether you've completed the tutorial, and your progress (which lore you've unlocked and which opponents you've beaten with each character). If you choose to save a lore image, it is saved to a "Magic Fight" album in your photo gallery. This information stays on your device and is never uploaded, so clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it resets your progress.

The Android app

The Android app doesn't use the internet at all: everything it needs is included in the app itself, and it asks for no permissions.

The web version

If you play in a web browser instead, the game is hosted by Vercel. Like most websites, Vercel automatically keeps short-term technical logs of visits, which can include your IP address. This information is not used to identify you and is not sold or shared for advertising. Your progress is still stored only in your browser.

Former leaderboard

Earlier test versions of the game had an optional online leaderboard. It is being retired with the latest update, and all of its data will be permanently deleted when that update is released.

Children

Magic Fight is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Because Magic Fight doesn't collect any data, there is nothing stored about you to delete. To erase your progress on Android, uninstall the app or go to Settings → Apps → Magic Fight → Storage → Clear storage. In a web browser, clear the site data for the game's website. Images you saved to your gallery stay there until you delete them yourself.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
